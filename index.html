How to use

  • Add: drag a piece out of the tray, or tap it to drop one in the middle.
  • Move: drag pieces. Drag on empty space to draw a box and select a group.
  • Flip / turn: double-tap a piece (two-sided counters flip, rods and blocks turn).
  • Exchange: select 10 matching place value counters or base 10 blocks and press Exchange to group them. Select 1 and press Exchange to break it into 10.
  • Remove: drag pieces back onto the toolbar, or press Delete.
  • Prepare a slide: set it up in normal view, then ⋯ → Save as starting layout and save the presentation. Every slideshow starts from that layout; ⋯ → Reset puts it back.
